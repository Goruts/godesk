## Free use

You can use, change and share Godesk at no cost when:

- **You use it personally.** Goruts grants every individual permission to use Godesk for personal, non-commercial purposes, whatever the size of the organization they work for. This permission is in addition to the license.
- **Your company is small.** The license allows use for the benefit of a company with fewer than 100 people (employees and independent contractors) and less than 1,000,000 USD (2019, adjusted for inflation) of revenue in the prior tax year. Freelancers, startups and small teams fall here.
- **You study, teach, research, or work for a nonprofit,** under the same terms.

## Commercial license

Using Godesk for the benefit of a company with 100 or more people, or with revenue over the limit above, requires a commercial license from Goruts. This includes the company's subsidiaries and affiliates.

To get one, write to **contacto@goruts.com** with your company's name and the number of people who will use Godesk.
